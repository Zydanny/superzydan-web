## My take

I use this all the time. It's great between washes, and honestly I use it pretty much exclusively. I really don't even take my car to the car wash anymore.

You just spray and wipe. In the video I take off some bug debris. I use it on my whole car, and after I finish I put a sealant on.

It doesn't take much. One bottle will clean your whole car multiple, multiple times. Highly recommend.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Spray and wipe, no rinse
- Takes off bug debris
- One bottle covers the whole car several times

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to keep their car clean between washes, or skip the car wash most of the time.
