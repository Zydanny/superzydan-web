## My take

This is my second Keurig. My old, bigger machine lasted me a couple of years before it finally went out, and I like this one better. It's small, it fits pretty much anywhere, and it's been great so far.

## How it works

On top you get four sizes, 6, 8, 10 and 12 oz, plus a strong brew button and an over ice button. Everything lights up when it's ready. I did a 12 oz strong brew, and it felt very fast.

It punches five holes into the K-Cup to spread more water through it. Some people say in reviews that they can't tell the difference, but for me the coffee tastes better.

It also doesn't stay powered on unless you're going to use it, so it saves electricity. And you can brew back to back. The next person doesn't have to wait for it to heat up again.

## What I liked

- Fast, and ready to go again right away
- Small enough to fit anywhere
- Four cup sizes, strong brew and over ice
- Doesn't sit there powered on all day

## Heads up

- The water tank has a filter you have to change about every two months.
- For a taller cup you have to pull out the drip tray.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- 6, 8, 10 and 12 oz brew sizes
- Strong brew and over ice settings
- Side water tank with a filter. I think you can move it to the back.
- Removable drip tray for taller cups
- Works with K-Cup pods

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants a quick cup without a full pot, especially in a house where people brew one after another in the morning.
