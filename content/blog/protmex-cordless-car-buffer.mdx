## My take

This is my first time owning a buffer or polishing tool like this, and man, this is nice. I've waxed cars by hand many, many times, and this is way better.

I put Meguiar's synthetic sealant on the car. Dab a little on the pad, spread it around, then turn it on and go. When I was done I wiped it all off with a microfiber cloth, and it came off really smooth and shiny.

I only used one battery for the whole car, and I don't think it was even low. I'll definitely be using it regularly on my cars. Definitely a good buy, and I'd suggest getting it.

**Heads up:** to turn it off, you hold the button down. It took me a second to figure that out.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Cordless, with two batteries in the box
- Eight speeds
- Pads that attach with Velcro
- A carry bag and a bunch of extra pads

## Who it's for

Anyone who waxes or seals their own car by hand and wants to save their arms, without paying for a pro polisher.
