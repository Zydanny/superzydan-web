## My take

Listen to me when I tell you this: this is the greatest tripod ever made for content creators.

It's MagSafe ready, so there's no squeezing your phone into a clamp, and it angles all the way down. The push-button remote is attached right on it, so you won't lose it. Push the little button and it opens up. It can be super small or super tall, and the base is super wide so you don't have to worry about it falling over.

It's magnetized on the back too. The selfie light is a separate accessory that snaps right on, opens up and turns on. It also comes with an extra magnetic ring you can stick on whatever you want. I put mine on a MagSafe battery, so if I'm worried about my phone dying, the battery snaps on too. It all just magnetizes together. No clamps.

Most tripods make you twist the legs or lock clamps to keep them open. Not this one. It just opens and closes however you want. And look how compact it is.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

Content creators with a MagSafe iPhone, or anyone who wants a tripod they can set up in a second.
