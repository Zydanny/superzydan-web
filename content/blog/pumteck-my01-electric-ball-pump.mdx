## My take

I just got this and it's absolutely amazing. Before this I was using a hand pump. My older son is into soccer, and we really couldn't tell if the ball was at the right PSI.

It has presets for a football, volleyball, basketball and soccer ball. Stick the needle in the ball and it tells you the current PSI. Set the PSI you want, hit start, and it airs up the ball and stops automatically when it gets there. How easy is that?

The first time we used it, our ball was at 3.3 PSI. A soccer ball is supposed to be between about 8.5 and 15.6. It was way underinflated and we never knew, so when he was practicing he wasn't getting the real feel of the ball.

If you're into sports and want your ball at the right PSI, this is a must-have.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Presets for football, volleyball, basketball and soccer
- Reads the ball's current PSI before you start
- Set a target PSI and it stops on its own
- Inflates and deflates

## Who it's for

Parents of kids in sports and anyone who's been guessing with a hand pump.
