## My take

You just heard what the Aula F99 Pro sounds like, and I really love the way it sounds. This is a beautiful keyboard. It has a comic book, anime kind of feel going on.

The keycaps and switches are hot-swappable, but honestly, why would you want to? I really like the way it looks already. It's heavy and weighted, and it feels really good.

You get three ways to connect it: Bluetooth, wired USB-C, or a wireless dongle that tucks into its own little slot on the keyboard. A switch flips between them.

It's a full-size keyboard with the number pad. There's a volume knob, and if you click it you can cycle through the light settings. And look at the feet. Most keyboards have flimsy little prop stands. These are so thick I don't think they're breaking anytime soon.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Full size, with a number pad
- Hot-swappable keycaps and switches
- Bluetooth, 2.4 GHz dongle or wired USB-C, with a slot to store the dongle
- 8,000 mAh battery
- Volume knob that also cycles the lighting
- Thick flip-out feet

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants a full-size wireless mechanical keyboard that sounds and looks great without spending a lot.
