Are you an Amazon Influencer who looks at your earnings report and wonders, "Why aren't my numbers what they should be?" You put in the work, you create the videos, but the commissions just aren't adding up. I might have the answer for you, and it's a silent income killer: **broken and unavailable product links.**

I'm an Amazon Influencer just like you, and I built a solution to this exact problem. Today, I'm excited to introduce you to the **Amazon Influencer Link Checker**, a Chrome extension I developed to protect our earnings and save us hours of manual work.

## _The Problem: The Silent Commission Killer_

_

Here's the frustrating reality: when a viewer clicks your video and the linked product is out of stock, unavailable, or the page is broken, you don't get credit for the sale. If someone purchases that product, you're not going to get credit for it. You have to go back to your Amazon storefront and relink it to the product to start getting credit again. With hundreds of videos, finding these broken links manually is nearly impossible.

## The Solution: An Automated Link Auditor

The Amazon Influencer Link Checker is a simple, one-button tool that automates this entire process. As I show in the video, you just click the "Scan Videos" button, and the extension gets to work.

-   It automatically scrolls through your entire storefront page to find every single video you have.
-   It then checks each associated product link one by one.
-   The scan runs in the background, and the best part is you can click off it and then click back on it, and it'll still keep going right where it left off!

### What the Results Mean

The extension gives you a clear, color-coded status for each product:

-   ✅ **Available:** The link is healthy, and you're ready to earn.
-   ❌ **Unavailable:** The product is out of stock or the page is gone. This link is losing you money and needs to be fixed immediately!
-   ⚠️ **Uncertain:** The product might have limited buying options or other issues. As I found with one of my own products, this means you "definitely need to relink this again to start getting paid for it."

## Key Features of the Extension

#### Automatic Availability Scan (Pro)

The core of the tool. It automatically scans your entire Amazon Influencer storefront, identifying the product links that are costing you money.

#### Easy CSV Export (Pro)

Once the scan is done, you can export the full report to a CSV file—basically, an Excel spreadsheet. It gives you the video title, the product link, and the availability status, so you can go through it that way and manage your updates efficiently.

#### FREE Parent ASIN Finder

This is a bonus tool for everyone. When you're on a product page with lots of variations (like a backpack with different colors), it can be hard to find the main product link that covers all options. This tool does it for you. You just click one button, and it finds the Parent ASIN for you to use in your videos, ensuring you get credit no matter which color a customer chooses.
