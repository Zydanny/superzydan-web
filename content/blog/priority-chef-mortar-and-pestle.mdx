I am super excited about this. Yes, really. I've wanted a mortar and pestle forever, and Priority Chef was kind enough to send me one to try. It's super heavy and high quality, and it comes with a beautiful pestle.

## Seasoning it, step by step

A new one has to be seasoned before you cook with it. They send you an instruction book, and I followed it exactly.

1. **Wash it.** Warm water, no soap. Carefully rinse out any dust or residue, do it a few times, then let it air dry.
2. **Grind rice.** Add a handful of rice and grind it down. Dump it out and repeat until the rice stays white. I did it about three times.
3. **Make a paste.** Four cloves of garlic, ground into a paste, then a teaspoon each of cumin, salt and pepper. It smells so good.
4. **Rinse and dry.** Rinse it out with water again and let it air dry. That's it, it's ready to use.
