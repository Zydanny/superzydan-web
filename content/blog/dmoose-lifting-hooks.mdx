## My take

I love these. To me they work way better than regular straps. They go around your wrist super easy, and then the metal hooks grab onto whatever you're pulling. If you don't have a lot of grip strength, they really help you do more weight.

They're great for deadlifts, but you can use them for anything. I use them on the pull-up bar with a wide grip or a close grip. You can also push the hooks back behind your wrist and still do push-ups or grab dumbbells with them on. They move in and out of your way as you need them.

I'd recommend them over straps any day.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Wrist wrap with a metal hook on each one
- Sold as a pair
- Works for deadlifts, rows and pull-ups, with wide or close grips
- The hook pushes back behind your wrist when you don't need it

## Who it's for

Anyone whose grip gives out before their back or legs do on pulling exercises, and anyone who finds straps slow to wrap.
