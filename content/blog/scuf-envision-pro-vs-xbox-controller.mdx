Look what came in today: the SCUF Envision Pro I custom built. I unboxed it, compared it to the design I ordered, and put it side by side with my regular Xbox controller.

## Unboxing

The box is premium, with hexagons all over it. The first thing you see inside says to download the software first, so I did that before anything else.

The controller feels heavy and premium, and the orange around the thumbsticks and the orange buttons came out exactly like I designed it.

In the box:

- A USB receiver. This is a big one for me. My wireless Xbox controller had a lot of latency, where I'd push a button and nothing would happen. The receiver should make it feel like it's plugged in.
- Extra buttons to replace the side and back buttons, if you'd rather have a more standard feel. I don't think I'll use those.
- A charger, and it's a premium-looking one.

## SCUF vs Xbox

- **Weight and grip:** the SCUF is so much heavier and feels so much better. The Xbox controller has a plasticky texture. The SCUF has more of a rubbery feel with really good grip on the back.
- **Triggers:** the recoil on the SCUF triggers is crazy, so much better. There's an adjustment on the back for FPS games. Flip it and the trigger stops short and clicks like a mouse button.
- **Face buttons:** so much better, almost like a tactile switch on a keyboard.
- **D-pad:** a huge difference. It feels like clicking a keyboard key with really good switches.
- **Thumbsticks:** about the same sound, but they feel tighter and move smoother.
- **Bumpers and top buttons:** a big improvement.
- **Battery:** I have to replace the batteries in my Xbox controller all the time. The SCUF charges over USB-C, and you can plug it in and keep playing.
- **Extra buttons:** five programmable buttons along the bottom that can do anything your computer can, like mute and unmute, plus back buttons and side buttons.

## What I liked

- Heavy, premium feel with a grippy back
- Trigger stops for FPS games
- Clicky buttons and d-pad
- USB-C charging instead of batteries
- The custom build matched my design exactly

## Heads up

- It's PC only.
- You need to install SCUF's software before you set it up.
- It costs a lot more than a standard Xbox controller.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Wireless with a USB receiver, or wired over USB-C
- Rechargeable battery with a charger in the box
- Adjustable trigger stops
- Five programmable G-keys, plus back and side buttons
- Replacement covers for the side and back buttons

## Who it's for

PC players who are tired of latency and batteries and want extra buttons. If you play casually, the standard Xbox controller still does the job for a lot less.

## My take

This thing is amazing. They did a great job matching my design, and every button feels better than my Xbox controller. I'll come back with how it plays once I've put real game time into it.
