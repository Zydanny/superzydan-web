## My take

I got this for my bedroom. Setup is about as easy as it gets. There are no tools or screws. The stand snaps into place on both sides, you run the cord through the hole, and the fan drops in and twists to lock. That's it.

For reference, I'm 5'7", and it comes up to my waist. Pretty tall, good size. There's a spot in the back to store the remote.

On speed one it's really quiet. By four it's kind of loud, honestly, but it won't bother you. It's a smooth airflow kind of loud, like white noise.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Tool-free stand that snaps together
- About waist high on someone who's 5'7"
- Remote that stores in the back
- Speeds from very quiet up to a white-noise level

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants a quiet bedroom fan that's easy to set up, especially if you sleep better with a little white noise.
