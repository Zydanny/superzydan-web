It was time to make the upgrade to a Hall Effect keyboard. I did a ton of research to find the right one. This is not your average TikTok-shop board, and nobody sponsored me. I looked at Wooting, Keychron, GravaStar, MelGeek, and a pile of others. When I landed on the Gateron collaboration with SIKAKEYB, it was just... that's the one.

I bought the Gateron × SIKAKEYB FNP75 HE myself. It comes in red, orange, and gold. Guess which one I picked.

<YouTube id="ZHqjBrN4cFA" />

## Unboxing

The box is heavy, which is a good sign. It ships in a real carry case, and the board has a built-in incline so you don't have to fake a tent. Aluminum case, 75% layout, USB-C, 8K polling, Rapid Trigger, and adjustable actuation: the Hall Effect stuff that actually matters for sweaty games. SIKAKEYB is known for the triple spacebar, and this one has it. Three independent switches instead of one wobbly bar.

Stock, it came with Gateron Delta switches. WASD and the stabilized keys use Delta Light; the rest of the board is custom themed to the color you pick, so mine are red. It already sounded good out of the box. Hot-swap, Gateron badge, and a little crystal dragon-heart emblem on the side that lights up. The included cable is a basic black USB-C. Fine, but it is not surviving on a red desk. That one's getting swapped.

## Swapping to Magnetic Jade Ultras

I still wanted the best, so I bought an extra set: Gateron Magnetic Jade Ultra, about $70 more. I was chasing that deeper, dockier mahjong sound. First I swapped four keys and compared WASD to JKL;. Barely a difference. Then I learned the WASD Deltas are a different SKU than the rest of the board, which explained some of the confusion, and I still couldn't tell them apart. I almost shipped the Ultras back.

For the love of the game I replaced every letter and the spacebar anyway. That's when it clicked. A full board of Jade Ultras is a little deeper and a tiny bit more clicky. Not night and day. Was it worth $70? Maybe, in the end. I kept them.

## Specs

- 75% Hall Effect
- USB-C wired, 8K polling
- Rapid Trigger and adjustable actuation
- Triple / split spacebar
- Aluminum case, 1,720 g
- Stock switches: Gateron Delta, with Delta Light on WASD
- Now running: Gateron Magnetic Jade Ultra
- Hot-swap
- Carry case included

If you want Hall Effect without guessing from a random shop listing, this is the board on my desk. Watch the unboxing and the switch tests, then grab the FNP75 HE if it looks like your color.
