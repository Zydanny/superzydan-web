## My take

My favorite thing about this shaker is that there's no little pesky ball you have to drop in to mix your protein.

I measured out 8 oz of water, added a scoop of protein, screwed the lid on and shook it. The unique shape of the bottle takes the liquid and the powder and really mixes them up well together. Then you open the lid and drink.

**Heads up:** screw the lid down tight before you shake it so it doesn't come off.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- No mixing ball. The bottle's shape does the mixing.
- Screw-on lid

## Who it's for

Anyone who's tired of washing and losing shaker balls.
