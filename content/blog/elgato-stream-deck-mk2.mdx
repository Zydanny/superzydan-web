## My take

I have mine set up with all my scenes for when I'm gaming, starting the stream and ending it. With one button I can turn off my Elgato Key Lights, or control their brightness. I have sound effects on it, a death counter that goes up every time I press it, buttons to make clips and mark highlight moments, and a record button. You can make as many folders as you want.

If you're a streamer or a content creator, or maybe even into music, this is a must-have. It makes everything so easy when you're streaming. You don't have to hunt for your mouse and move things around. It's just the push of a button. It's been amazing, and I would highly, highly suggest getting one.

## Key specs

- 15 customizable keys
- Unlimited folders of buttons
- Controls scenes, recording, clips, sound effects and Elgato lights

## Who it's for

Streamers and content creators who are tired of alt-tabbing and clicking around mid-stream.
