## My take

The makers of Taco vs Burrito sent us a copy, and we just got through playing a few games. It's a great, fun game for 2 to 4 players, and it's super easy to learn. It comes with instructions, plus a code you can scan to watch a how-to video.

Everybody gets five cards and the youngest goes first. You build your taco or burrito with food cards for points, and the action cards are where it gets wild: spicy doubles your points, a food fight makes everyone flip a card, and the safety inspector can wipe out someone's whole burrito. My son lost all his burrito points to that one.

My son did not win this game and he was not excited about losing. Between my wife and me, she won. He still had fun during the game, though, and last time we played, when he won, it was the best time ever.

It's a really fun game. If you have a family, or you're just doing a game night with friends, give it a try.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- 2 to 4 players
- Instructions plus a scannable link to a how-to video
- Five-card hands, with point cards and action cards

## Who it's for

Families with kids, and anyone who wants a quick game that's easy to learn.
