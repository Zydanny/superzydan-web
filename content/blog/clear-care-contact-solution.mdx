## My take

This is the Clear Care twin pack we use all the time in this house. It comes with two bottles and one of the little lens cases.

If you've never used it before, know this: it's not like regular contact lens solution. Once your contacts go in, you have to leave them for a set amount of time before you can wear them, or it will burn. The directions say to rinse your lenses with the solution, fill the case to the line, put the lenses in, tighten the lid, don't shake it, and soak for at least 6 hours.

So if you need your contacts back within 6 hours, this is not the solution for you. I use it when I'm going to bed and I'm done for the day. Once they're in there, there's no using them until it's done, because it will burn your eyes. Trust me. I learned the hard way.

Other than that, it cleans really great. Very effective.

## Key specs

From the box and the video:

- Twin pack with a lens case
- Rinse, fill the case to the line, and soak for at least 6 hours
- No rubbing required

## Who it's for

Contact wearers who take their lenses out at night and want them really clean by morning.
