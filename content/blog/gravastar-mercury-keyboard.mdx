## My take

You just heard what the keyboard from the future sounds like. It's not actually from the future, but it looks very futuristic. And unlike other budget keyboards, it's not made of plastic. It's aluminum.

As a content creator who keeps growing, I get offered a lot of products these days, and rarely am I as excited as I was about this one. Mainly because of the looks. It's not even a keyboard, it's a piece of art.

This is not for someone who wants to be low-key. If you have this keyboard, people are going to ask where you got it, who made it and what kind of keyboard it is.

The stands are built into the design itself, so you can raise or lower it. There's a volume knob, and a cool little symbol button to change the colors. It connects three ways: a USB dongle (which I'm using, and it has a spot to store it), Bluetooth, or wired USB-C.

If you want a showstopper for your gaming setup, this is it.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Aluminum body
- 2.4 GHz dongle, Bluetooth or wired USB-C, with a spot to store the dongle
- Built-in stands to raise or lower it
- Volume knob and a button to change the colors

## Who it's for

Anyone building a setup people will notice. If you'd rather have something quiet and plain, look elsewhere.
