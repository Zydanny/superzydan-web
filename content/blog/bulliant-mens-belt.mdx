## My take

I opened this on camera, so this is my first look. The packaging is pretty nice. They have several designs, and this is the one I picked, with a little pattern on it.

It's a ratchet belt, so there are no holes to worry about. You slide it in and it clicks until it's tight. To take it off, pull the little switch on the buckle up and it slides right out.

It seems like a great belt, and I'll absolutely be using it.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Ratchet buckle with no holes. It clicks to tighten.
- A switch on the buckle releases it
- Several designs to choose from
- Comes in a gift box

## Who it's for

Anyone who's tired of belts that are always between two holes.
