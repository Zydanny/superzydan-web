## **The Battle We All Face (And the Things I Can’t Stop Thinking About)**

We’ve all been there—staring at our screens, wallets in hand, debating whether that _one thing_ is a **need** or just a burning **want**. As much as we try to be responsible, sometimes the thrill of the hunt (or the dopamine hit of a new obsession) wins. **Today, I’m breaking down three things I’m _dying_ to have—and whether they’re worth the hype (and the cash).**

---

## **1\. Labubu: The Mischievous, Must-Have Blind Box Sensation**

**First up**, if you’ve scrolled through TikTok or Instagram lately, you’ve probably seen **Labubu**—the toothy, pointy-eared monster taking over Pop Mart’s blind boxes. Created by Hong Kong artist **Kasing Lung**, this fuzzy little troublemaker is part of _The Monsters_ series, inspired by Nordic folklore.

![Labubu](/blog/need-vs-want-episode-1/labubu.webp)

### **Why Everyone’s Obsessed**

-   **Blind Box Thrill**: Each [$27.99 box](https://www.popmart.com) is a surprise—will you get a common Labubu or the ultra-rare **"secret" version**? The gamble is addictive, and resellers mark them up to **hundreds** for rare finds.
-   **Celeb-Approved**: Blackpink’s **Lisa** and Rihanna have been spotted accessorizing with Labubu, turning it into a **luxury fashion flex**.
-   **Emotional Connection**: Fans say Labubu “heals their inner child”—adorable, collectible, and just a little bit weird.

### **The Hunt for the Secret Labubu**

**Here’s the thing**—scoring the **secret variant** is like winning the lottery. Restocks sell out in _seconds_, and fans camp outside Pop Mart stores overnight. **Pro tip**: Join **r/labubu** for restock alerts—or accept that your wallet might hate you after [eBay markups](https://www.ebay.com).

**Verdict**: Pure **want**—but oh, what a fun one.

## **2\. Praying Mantis Pet: The Ultimate Low-Maintenance Predator**

**Next on my list?** Forget cats and dogs—I’ve been eyeing a **praying mantis** as my next pet. These fierce little hunters are surprisingly easy to care for, but picking the right species is key.

![orchid-mantis2](/blog/need-vs-want-episode-1/orchid-mantis2.webp)

### **Top Mantis Species for Beginners**

-   **Chinese Mantis**: Hardy and common, great for first-timers (but **cannibalistic**—keep them solo!).
-   **Ghost Mantis**: A **communal** species (less likely to eat each other) with a leaf-like camouflage.
-   **Orchid Mantis**: Stunning pink-and-white looks, but needs **higher humidity** for molting.

### **Care Basics**

-   **Habitat**: A small, ventilated tank (3x their height) with sticks for molting ([check this starter kit](https://www.amazon.com)).
-   **Food**: Live crickets, moths, or fruit flies (no veggies—they’re **strict carnivores**).
-   **Pro Tip**: Avoid buying hatchlings—opt for **3rd instar or older** for easier care.

**Verdict**: A **need** for bug lovers, a **want** for the squeamish.

## **3\. JNCO Jeans Shorts: The ’90s Comeback We Didn’t Know We Needed**

**Finally**, nostalgia is hitting hard, and JNCO’s **Twin Cannon shorts** (in black and blue) are calling my name. These ultra-baggy, skate-style shorts are a throwback to the **late ’90s/Y2K** era—and they’re back in a big way.

![Twin Cannon](/blog/need-vs-want-episode-1/twin-cannon.webp)

### **Why They’re Trending**

-   **Statement Piece**: Not just shorts, but a **fashion flex** with massive pockets and that iconic wide-leg fit.
-   **Resale Craze**: Vintage JNCOs go for **hundreds**, making [new drops](https://www.jnco.com) a (slightly) cheaper alternative.
-   **Comfort Factor**: Breathable, loose, and perfect for summer.

**Verdict**: A **want**—but if you love streetwear, maybe a _justifiable_ one.

## **Final Thoughts: The Fine Line Between Need and Want**

**At the end of the day**, **needs** keep us alive—but **wants** make life fun. Whether it’s a rare Labubu, a pet mantis, or those iconic JNCO shorts, the thrill of the chase is half the joy. **So, what’s your latest guilty-pleasure want?** Drop yours in the comments—no judgment here!
