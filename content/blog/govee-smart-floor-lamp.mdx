## My take

This is actually my second one. I gave my first one to my son and he loves it, so I had to get a new one. I do recommend it.

In the box you get the plug, three poles, the base and the light. The poles slide into each other about halfway, then you screw them together with the screws and screwdriver that come in the bag. That slides into the base with two more screws. Click the cable in, push it down into the base, then squeeze the cord into the channel all the way up.

There's a little holder for the remote that you can put anywhere on the back, so you don't lose it.

It has tons of scenes. Turned toward the wall, it lights the wall up really well. I had it on rainbow mode, just cycling through colors.

## Key specs

From what I show in the video:

- Three-piece pole, base and light bar
- Screws and a screwdriver included
- Cord hides in a channel up the pole
- Remote with a holder that clips on the back
- Lots of scenes and color modes, including rainbow

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to light up a corner or a wall in a game room, bedroom or living room.
